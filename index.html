About this calculator

Fine-Gray competing-risks model with age and natural splines of eGFR and log 24-hour urine protein, fitted in patients with renal AL amyloidosis evaluated at the Boston University Amyloidosis Center, 1994-2023 (time zero = first center visit, when the labs are drawn). Death without ESKD is the competing event. Internally validated (bootstrap and repeated cross-validation over 20 imputations); not yet externally validated.

Estimates are population averages from three baseline values. They do not account for treatment or hematologic response. Not a substitute for clinical judgement. Nothing you enter leaves this page.
